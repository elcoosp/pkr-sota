## Decision

Plan §3 rule: ship iff `Δ_LBR <= -2*SE_combined` **AND** H2H ≥ +2·SE.
- Δ_LBR = -345.79, threshold = -268.2 → **PASS**
- H2H t = 5.28 / 7.02, both > 2 → **PASS**

**K=7 is the new base.** The K=7 action abstraction (NUM_ACTION_BUCKETS
6→7, BET_SIZINGS [0.5,1,2]→[0.5,1,2,3], bucket 6 = jam) is kept.

## In-sample BR (for reference only)

| 25M | 8148 | 127 | 5000 |

K=7 in-sample BR at 100M is 7922. K=6 (overnight-v4) was 6215 at 100M.
The 27% gap is expected: K=7's action space is strictly larger, so
the same in-sample greedy exploiter has more places to profit. This is
**not** a strength regression -- it is a different abstract game. The
K=7 LBR (-163) and H2H (+0.96/+1.28) are the strength readings that
matter, and both improve on K=6.

## LBR context

K=6 V5 LBR was 183 ± 84 (20k hands, seed 42). K=7 is -163 ± 104. Both
are "competent" band on the project's scale (<500 mbb; superhuman ≈ 50).
K=7 is ~2.6 SE better than K=6. The absolute improvement is real but
the SW estimate is wide; the H2H (independent of LBR) confirms the
direction at t≈5–7.
