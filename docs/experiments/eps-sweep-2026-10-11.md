# ε (exploration) sweep at 20M iters (2026-10-11)

6-run A/B of `PKR_EXPLORE_EPSILON in {0.01, 0.002, 0.0005}` x 2 seeds,
20M iters each, cap 20M, all other flags matching the K=7 baseline.
LBR at 20k hands, seed 42 for every checkpoint.

| run | LBR | ±SE |
|---|---:|---:|
| ε=0.01 seed 42 | 422.43 | 116.52 |
| ε=0.01 seed 43 | 193.03 | 114.33 |
| ε=0.002 seed 42 | 214.62 | 116.31 |
| ε=0.002 seed 43 | 227.12 | 114.71 |
| ε=0.0005 seed 42 | 178.46 | 115.27 |
| ε=0.0005 seed 43 | (running) | — |

(K=7 100M baseline at ε=0.01: −162.79 ± 104.49, for reference only.)

## What the numbers say

By group mean, and by looking at each seed separately:

| ε | seed 42 | seed 43 | mean | spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.01 | 422.4 | 193.0 | 307.7 | 229.4 |
| 0.002 | 214.6 | 227.1 | 220.9 | 12.5 |
| 0.0005 | 178.5 | pending | — | — |

Observations:

1. **ε=0.01 has a huge seed-to-seed spread (229 mbb).** The other
   configurations are much tighter (ε=0.002: 12 mbb). This is itself
   informative: at high ε the run's outcome is dominated by which
   exploration draws happen in the first few million iterations.

2. **On seed 42, lower ε is monotonically better** (422 -> 214 -> 178
   as ε drops from 0.01 to 0.0005). But seed 43 for ε=0.01 was
   already in the same band as the lower-ε runs, so the "win" at
   seed 42 mostly reflects how BAD ε=0.01's seed 42 happened to be.

3. **The whole comparison is at 20M iters.** The K=7 reference is at
   100M, where the same metric is −163. None of these 20M readings
   is converged. **This A/B measures ε's effect on the convergence
   RATE, not on the final fixed point.** A 20M result does not
   predict the 100M ordering.

## What to do with it

- **Record the numbers.** They are the only ε A/B the project has.
- **Do NOT flip the default on this alone.** The plan's §3 rule wants
  a 100M A/B with 2 seeds, and the Leduc signal (ε=0.002 better by
  ~35% at convergence) says nothing directly about NLHE at 20M.
- If a 100M A/B is run: ε=0.002 is the natural candidate to compare
  against the shipped 0.01 (its 20M reading is tight and does not
  look worse, unlike 0.01 which was wild on one seed).
- ε=0.0005 is the more aggressive arm. Its seed-42 reading (178) is
  the best single 20M number, but it is one seed and the difference
  from ε=0.002 seed 42 (215) is ~0.3 SE.

## Leduc reference

`docs/experiments/leduc-cfr-grid-2026-10-10.md`: ε=0.002 gives ~35%
lower exploitability than 0.01 on Leduc at 3M iters, mean over 2
seeds. Consistent with the direction here, but a different game and
a much smaller one.

## Recommended next step (not queued)

A 100M A/B of {0.01, 0.002}, 2 seeds each, LBR + duplicate H2H per
the plan §3 rule. ~12h compute. This is the only way to decide
whether to change the default before the next production retrain.
