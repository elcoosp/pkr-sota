## fast7 bugs found and fixed

P1-a wiring regressed 5M reading by +3400 mbb. Bisect traced to
Fast7Evaluator. Three bugs, all fixed:

1. Card encoding mismatch: fast7 used rank*4+suit; every other
   site uses suit*13+rank. Fixed.
2. Flush path missed straight flushes (top-5-by-id vs all C(m,5)).
   Fixed.
3. COMBOS_7_5[..6] for m=6 read out-of-range slots. Fixed.

Parity tests had been silently skipping (relative paths under
nextest). Fixed find_rank_table() with CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR.
Added 4 targeted regressions: fast7::fast7_bug_regressions::*.
